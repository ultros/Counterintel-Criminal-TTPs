# Terminology atlas

This is the public companion to **Counterintel & Criminal TTPs**: a 500-term research taxonomy arranged into **25 thematic chapters** (20 entries each). Stable CP identifiers are for cross-referencing, not diagnostic or legal classification.

**Publication status:** All 500 term titles are indexed. Two terms per chapter (50 total) have public editorial-preview definitions. The complete book manuscript and remaining definitions have deliberately not been published here.

**Classification:** `E` recognized cross-disciplinary term (provisional); `D` descriptive working term; `Q` qualified/contested. Individual scholarly citation checks are incomplete.
