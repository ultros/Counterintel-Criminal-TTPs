## Short definition

A benefit is offered, requested, or received to improperly influence official or entrusted action.

## Evidentiary interpretation

- **What the label describes:** A behavioral, investigative, or analytical concept, not an identified offender or a verified criminal event.
- **What the label does not establish:** Intent, causality, collaboration, diagnosis, or legal liability.
- **Evidence to assess:** Contemporaneous records, independent corroboration, chronology, participants' information, and contradictory observations.
- **Alternative explanation:** Consider a credible competing or innocent account of the same conduct.
- **Falsification:** Specify a concrete result that would require abandoning or revising the explanation.
