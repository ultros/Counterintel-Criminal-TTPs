# Public terminology definitions

**50 editorial previews**, two from each of 25 chapters. For all other identifiers see the [500-term index](ATLAS-INDEX.md). This is a research draft; term-level sources are undergoing review.

## 01. Reactive traps & manufactured threats

### [CP-001: Induced-Threat Provocation via Causal Concealment](entries/CP-001.md) · `D`

A warning conceals that the warned person's intervention is what could trigger the predicted reaction.

### [CP-011: False Choice Architecture](entries/CP-011.md) · `D`

Available options are arranged to conceal a reasonable alternative or make every visible option costly.

## 02. Judgment, bias & decision errors

### [CP-021: Confirmation Bias](entries/CP-021.md) · `E`

Information supporting an existing belief receives more attention or weight than disconfirming evidence.

### [CP-031: Scarcity Effect](entries/CP-031.md) · `E`

Limited availability increases perceived value or urgency, sometimes independent of merit.

## 03. Memory, perception & witness reliability

### [CP-041: Misinformation Effect](entries/CP-041.md) · `E`

Misleading post-event information alters later recollection of an event.

### [CP-051: Confidence Inflation](entries/CP-051.md) · `E`

Feedback or repeated identification may increase certainty without increasing original accuracy.

## 04. Narrative control & information distortion

### [CP-061: Gaslighting](entries/CP-061.md) · `E`

Manipulation leads a person to doubt their perceptions, experiences, or understanding of events.

### [CP-071: Firehose of Falsehood](entries/CP-071.md) · `E`

High-volume, repetitive, multi-channel falsehoods can distort what an audience accepts.

## 05. Groups, reputations & social influence

### [CP-081: Triangulation](entries/CP-081.md) · `E`

A third party is drawn into an existing relationship or conflict to shift its dynamics.

### [CP-091: Ostracism](entries/CP-091.md) · `E`

Exclusion from a group functions as social punishment or control.

## 06. Coercion, threats & compliance

### [CP-101: Coercive Control](entries/CP-101.md) · `E`

A repeated pattern restricts another person's autonomy through intimidation, monitoring, isolation, or deprivation.

### [CP-111: Economic Coercion](entries/CP-111.md) · `E`

Control over earnings, debt, employment, or necessities constrains another person's choices.

## 07. Abusive relationships & interpersonal control

### [CP-121: Isolation Tactic](entries/CP-121.md) · `D`

Relationships with friends, family, or advisers are restricted to reduce outside reality checks.

### [CP-131: Rationalization](entries/CP-131.md) · `E`

Explanations make objectionable actions seem reasonable after the fact.

## 08. Identity, authority & impersonation

### [CP-141: Authority Spoofing](entries/CP-141.md) · `D`

Someone presents a fabricated official capacity to obtain deference or compliance.

### [CP-151: Impostor Syndrome](entries/CP-151.md) · `E`

A person's unwarranted doubts about their own legitimacy or competence; not evidence of actual fraud.

## 09. Confidence games & fraudulent persuasion

### [CP-161: Confidence Trick](entries/CP-161.md) · `E`

A victim's trust is cultivated and then exploited for an unauthorized gain.

### [CP-171: Fake Scarcity Offer](entries/CP-171.md) · `D`

An opportunity is represented as almost unavailable to discourage comparison or verification.

## 10. Money, assets & economic leverage

### [CP-181: Embezzlement](entries/CP-181.md) · `E`

Entrusted money or property is wrongfully appropriated by someone with legitimate access.

### [CP-191: Invoice Redirection](entries/CP-191.md) · `E`

Payment instructions are falsified to divert a legitimate transaction.

## 11. Sexual exploitation, grooming & consent

### [CP-201: Sexual Coercion](entries/CP-201.md) · `E`

Pressure, threats, or misuse of power undermines freely given sexual consent.

### [CP-211: Sex Trafficking](entries/CP-211.md) · `E`

People are recruited, transported, or controlled for commercial sexual exploitation under applicable law.

## 12. Criminal networks, roles & internal incentives

### [CP-221: Organized Crime Enterprise](entries/CP-221.md) · `E`

A continuing association coordinates criminal activity for material benefit, influence, or control.

### [CP-231: Straw Person](entries/CP-231.md) · `E`

A nominal holder or participant acts on behalf of an undisclosed interested party.

## 13. Corruption, office misuse & institutional capture

### [CP-241: Bribery](entries/CP-241.md) · `E`

A benefit is offered, requested, or received to improperly influence official or entrusted action.

### [CP-251: Process as Punishment](entries/CP-251.md) · `D`

The burden of a procedure itself becomes a sanction regardless of its eventual outcome.

## 14. Stalking, harassment & intrusion

### [CP-261: Stalking](entries/CP-261.md) · `E`

A repeated course of unwanted conduct creates fear or substantial distress under applicable definitions.

### [CP-271: Property Interference](entries/CP-271.md) · `D`

Tampering with or obstructing personal property becomes a means of intimidation or control.

## 15. Threat assessment, aggression & escalation

### [CP-281: Threat Assessment](entries/CP-281.md) · `E`

Structured information gathering evaluates the possibility of harm and suitable protective responses.

### [CP-291: Protective Factor](entries/CP-291.md) · `E`

A condition that can lessen risk or buffer against adverse outcomes.

## 16. Crime scene behavior & offender inference

### [CP-301: Modus Operandi](entries/CP-301.md) · `E`

The methods used to carry out an offense, which may adapt as circumstances change.

### [CP-311: Organized Scene Label](entries/CP-311.md) · `Q`

An older profiling shorthand that can exaggerate the diagnostic meaning of planning or order.

## 17. Interviewing, questioning & confessions

### [CP-321: Cognitive Interview](entries/CP-321.md) · `E`

A structured witness-interview method uses memory-supporting prompts to improve retrieval without supplying answers.

### [CP-331: Interrogative Suggestibility](entries/CP-331.md) · `E`

A person is susceptible to adopting misleading suggestions during questioning.

## 18. Evidence, timelines & provenance

### [CP-341: Chain of Custody](entries/CP-341.md) · `E`

A documented history of evidence collection, control, transfer, storage, and handling supports integrity assessment.

### [CP-351: Evidence Contamination](entries/CP-351.md) · `E`

Material is altered or mixed through collection, handling, or exposure to unrelated information.

## 19. Digital deception & social engineering

### [CP-361: Social Engineering](entries/CP-361.md) · `E`

Human trust, attention, or organizational routines are exploited to obtain access or information.

### [CP-371: Quishing](entries/CP-371.md) · `E`

A malicious or misleading QR-code interaction directs a person toward phishing or another unsafe action.

## 20. Cybercrime incentives & digital identity

### [CP-381: Account Takeover](entries/CP-381.md) · `E`

An unauthorized actor gains control of an existing digital account.

### [CP-391: Data Broker Exposure](entries/CP-391.md) · `E`

Commercial collection and resale of personal information create opportunities for unauthorized use.

## 21. Strategic deception, influence & intelligence concepts

### [CP-401: Reflexive Control](entries/CP-401.md) · `E`

Selected information is supplied to influence the decisions an opponent chooses as their own.

### [CP-411: Disinformation](entries/CP-411.md) · `E`

False or misleading information is deliberately disseminated with intent to deceive.

## 22. Platforms, online mobs & information ecosystems

### [CP-421: Astroturfing](entries/CP-421.md) · `E`

Coordinated messaging is disguised as spontaneous grassroots support.

### [CP-431: Filter Bubble](entries/CP-431.md) · `E`

Personalized information selection can narrow what viewpoints a user encounters.

## 23. Belief systems, high-control groups & radicalization

### [CP-441: High-Control Group](entries/CP-441.md) · `E`

A group restricts members' choices, contacts, information, or exit through sustained social pressure.

### [CP-451: Identity Fusion](entries/CP-451.md) · `E`

A person's personal identity becomes strongly integrated with a group identity.

## 24. Trauma, victim experience & recovery

### [CP-461: Acute Stress Response](entries/CP-461.md) · `E`

Immediate physiological and cognitive reactions follow a threatening or overwhelming event.

### [CP-471: Secondary Victimization](entries/CP-471.md) · `E`

Institutional or social responses cause additional harm after the original incident.

## 25. Research discipline, ethics & analytical safeguards

### [CP-481: Falsifiability](entries/CP-481.md) · `E`

A proposition must allow observations that could demonstrate it is wrong.

### [CP-491: Ecological Fallacy](entries/CP-491.md) · `E`

Group-level statistics are improperly applied to the behavior of a particular individual.
