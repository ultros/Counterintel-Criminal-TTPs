# Contributing and editorial corrections

Thank you for improving the accuracy of this research glossary. The project prioritizes **precise definitions, evidence, and falsifiability** over large counts or dramatic labels.

## Submit a correction

1. Identify the stable term ID, e.g. `CP-001`.
2. Quote the exact claim or phrase to correct.
3. State the replacement and explain what evidence supports it.
4. Provide a primary source, a peer-reviewed publication, or clearly labeled disciplinary guidance where possible.
5. Identify a competing explanation or a limitation of the proposed definition.

## Accepted proposals

- Clearer distinctions between neighboring terms.
- Fixes to unsupported or overstated claims.
- Source additions with precise locator/page/URL and publication details.
- Cross-reference suggestions and factual editorial corrections.

## Outside scope

- Unverified accusations about named individuals or organizations.
- Private case material, identifying victim information, or confidential law-enforcement data.
- Attempts to use the catalog as a clinical diagnostic rubric or criminal guilt score.
- Instructions for perpetrating harm, coercion, harassment, fraud, or evidence fabrication.

## Version and identifier policy

Identifiers `CP-001`–`CP-500` are stable. Update names/definitions without silent renumbering. When merging terms, retain redirects and explain the reasons in the changelog. New term proposals should not displace existing IDs.

**Rights:** Opening an issue or pull request does not automatically license your unrelated copyright-protected work for reuse. Only submit material you are permitted to share.
